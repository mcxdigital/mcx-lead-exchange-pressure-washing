Policies & Privacy.

Clear information about estimate requests, referrals to independent service providers, and how your information is handled.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Who operates this website

LocalQuote is a consumer-facing lead-generation brand operated by MCX Digital (“MCX,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) through MCX Lead Exchange. The service helps consumers submit requests for services and connects those requests with independent contractors or service providers. MCX is not necessarily the company that performs the requested work. Services are performed by independent contractors or other service providers, not by MCX unless expressly stated otherwise. The service name displayed at the top of this page identifies the type of service being requested; it is not a separate legal business or service provider.

2. Information we collect

When you submit a request, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, telephone number, email address, property location or ZIP code, property type, requested service, preferred timeframe, and project details. We may also receive basic technical information associated with your visit, such as browser or device information, where provided by our website or service providers.

Please do not submit payment-card details, passwords, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through the request form.

3. How we use your information

We use request information to receive and process your service inquiry; determine whether to perform additional qualification; classify the inquiry as raw/unqualified or personally qualified, as applicable; offer and sell the lead to an independent service provider; maintain records of submissions and lead transactions; prevent misuse; and operate and improve the service. MCX may not personally contact you or verify the details you submit.

4. Lead generation, sale, and sharing with service providers

By submitting a service request through this website, you understand and agree that this request is handled through LocalQuote, a lead-generation brand operated by MCX Digital, and that the information you submit may be shared with and sold to one independent contractor or service provider selected by MCX for the service you requested. MCX is not the contractor performing the work and is not necessarily the party that will contact you about the project.

MCX may review your submission and may, but is not required to, manually contact you, verify details, or perform additional qualification. Some inquiries may be sold as raw, unqualified leads based on the information submitted. Other inquiries may receive additional review and be sold as personally qualified leads. The lead is represented to the contractor according to the level of review actually performed; a submission is not guaranteed to be verified, complete, exclusive, or qualified.

MCX may receive compensation when a lead is sold or provided to a service provider. Once your inquiry is delivered, the receiving contractor decides whether to contact you, how to follow up, and whether to offer an estimate or services. A contractor's receipt of your information does not guarantee that the contractor will contact you, that you will receive an estimate, or that any work will be performed.

Each service request is sold and provided to one contractor only. MCX does not distribute the same submission to multiple contractors. The contractor receiving your information may choose whether to contact you about the service you requested. Do not submit a request if you do not agree to this lead-sharing and sales process.

After a contractor receives your information, that contractor handles it under its own privacy practices and is responsible for its own decisions, communications, estimates, and service arrangements. MCX does not control whether or how a contractor follows up.

5. Technology providers and other disclosures

We may use third-party technology and business-service providers to host this website, collect form submissions, store records, protect the service, and support communications. They may process information on our behalf for those purposes. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights and safety, or address fraud or misuse.

6. Calls, texts, and email

By submitting your request, you agree that the one contractor receiving your lead may use the contact information you provided to contact you about the service you requested. MCX may also contact you if reasonably necessary to process or clarify your submission, but it is not required to do so. Contractors decide independently whether to follow up.

This notice concerns follow-up about the specific service inquiry you submitted. It is not consent to unrelated marketing or recurring promotional messages. Automated calls or texts, prerecorded or artificial-voice calls, and other communications subject to additional legal consent requirements must comply with those requirements; submitting a service request does not override them. You may ask a contractor not to contact you further about the inquiry.

7. Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information handled through the service. No website, storage system, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the inquiry, document permissions and referrals, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. We then delete, de-identify, or securely dispose of it when appropriate.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to ask questions about information you submitted or to request correction or deletion. We will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law, subject to information we must retain for legitimate business, security, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes. Some information already provided to an independent service provider may also need to be requested directly from that provider.

9. Website terms and service estimates

Website content is provided for general information. Submitting a request does not create a service contract, confirm an appointment, guarantee provider availability, or guarantee a particular price or result. Any estimate, scope of work, schedule, cleaning method, payment terms, and service agreement must be confirmed with the independent provider before work begins.

Service descriptions are illustrative. The suitability of a cleaning method and the expected results depend on the property, surface material, condition, access, and other project-specific factors. The independent provider is responsible for evaluating the property and explaining its proposed work.

10. Third-party websites

This website may link to third-party websites or services. We do not operate or control those sites and are not responsible for their content, security, or privacy practices. Review their terms and privacy notices before providing information.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the service, technology, or operating arrangements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain additional permission for material changes.

12. Contact

For questions about this website or privacy requests, contact MCX Digital at help@mcxdigital.net.
