GULF COAST EXTERIOR CARE

Policies & Privacy.

Clear information about estimate requests, referrals to independent service providers, and how your information is handled.

Pre-launch review: This policy describes the intended lead-request process. Before accepting live inquiries, MCX must confirm the final consent wording and referral workflow, and have the policy and applicable calling/texting practices reviewed by qualified counsel.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Who operates this website

Gulf Coast Exterior Care is a consumer-facing website and service-request brand operated by MCX Digital (“MCX,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). MCX operates a lead-generation and referral service. We are not necessarily the company that performs the exterior-cleaning work. Service work is performed by independent contractors or other service providers, not by MCX unless expressly stated otherwise.

2. Information we collect

When you submit a request, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, telephone number, email address, property location or ZIP code, property type, requested service, preferred timeframe, and project details. We may also receive basic technical information associated with your visit, such as browser or device information, where provided by our website or service providers.

Please do not submit payment-card details, passwords, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through the request form.

3. How we use your information

We use request information to review and, where appropriate, qualify your inquiry; contact you about the service you requested; clarify project details; identify a potentially suitable independent service provider; obtain and record any required permission to make a referral; maintain business records; prevent misuse; and operate and improve the service.

4. Referrals and sharing with service providers

We do not provide your identifying contact information to an independent contractor for the purpose of selling or fulfilling your inquiry unless you have first been clearly told which provider would receive it and have affirmatively authorized that specific referral. Our intended process is to receive your request, review it, identify a potential provider, explain the proposed referral, and obtain your authorization before sharing your contact details.

MCX operates a commercial lead-generation service and may receive compensation from a service provider for a lead or referral you authorize. The provider may then contact you about the service you requested. We will explain the intended recipient and the information to be shared before asking you to authorize the referral.

For the initial program, MCX intends to prioritize exclusive referrals. Whether a particular referral is exclusive, and whether your information could be shared with any additional provider, must be explained before you authorize it. Permission to share information with one named provider does not authorize sharing it with other providers.

After a provider receives your information, that provider handles it under its own privacy practices and is responsible for its own communications and service arrangements.

5. Technology providers and other disclosures

We may use third-party technology and business-service providers to host this website, collect form submissions, store records, protect the service, and support communications. They may process information on our behalf for those purposes. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights and safety, or address fraud or misuse.

6. Calls, texts, and email

If you submit a request, MCX may contact you using the details you provide to discuss that specific inquiry. If you separately authorize a referral, the identified service provider may contact you about that request. A service request or referral authorization is not blanket permission for unrelated marketing, recurring promotional messages, or automated calls or texts. Any additional consent required for those communications will be requested separately and described at the point of collection.

You may tell MCX or the service provider that you no longer wish to be contacted about your inquiry. This does not affect communications that are legally permitted or necessary to complete an existing transaction or address a dispute.

7. Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information handled through the service. No website, storage system, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the inquiry, document permissions and referrals, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. We then delete, de-identify, or securely dispose of it when appropriate.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to ask questions about information you submitted or to request correction or deletion. We will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law, subject to information we must retain for legitimate business, security, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes. Some information already provided to an independent service provider may also need to be requested directly from that provider.

9. Website terms and service estimates

Website content is provided for general information. Submitting a request does not create a service contract, confirm an appointment, guarantee provider availability, or guarantee a particular price or result. Any estimate, scope of work, schedule, cleaning method, payment terms, and service agreement must be confirmed with the independent provider before work begins.

Service descriptions are illustrative. The suitability of a cleaning method and the expected results depend on the property, surface material, condition, access, and other project-specific factors. The independent provider is responsible for evaluating the property and explaining its proposed work.

10. Third-party websites

This website may link to third-party websites or services. We do not operate or control those sites and are not responsible for their content, security, or privacy practices. Review their terms and privacy notices before providing information.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the service, technology, or operating arrangements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain additional permission for material changes.

12. Contact

For questions about this website or privacy requests, contact MCX Digital at help@mcxdigital.net.
