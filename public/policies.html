GULF COAST EXTERIOR CARE

Policies & Privacy.

Clear information about this website, estimate requests, and how information is handled.

Pre-launch policy draft. This page is a working draft and must be completed and reviewed before the estimate form is activated or the website is promoted. Business contact details, final operating arrangements, and any applicable legal requirements still need to be confirmed.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Who operates this website

Gulf Coast Exterior Care is a consumer-facing service brand operated by MCX Digital (“we,” “us,” or “our”). The website helps people in the Lake Charles area and Southwest Louisiana request information about exterior-cleaning services. Gulf Coast Exterior Care is a service brand; it is not intended to imply that MCX Digital personally performs every service requested through the website.

2. Information you may provide

If you submit an estimate request after the form is activated, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, property type, requested service, property location, and details you choose to provide about the project. Please do not submit payment-card information, passwords, or other sensitive personal information through this website.

3. How information may be used

Information submitted through an estimate request may be used to review your inquiry, contact you about the requested service, clarify project details, determine whether a service provider may be suitable, and manage the request. We may also use limited information to maintain records, prevent misuse, and operate and improve the service.

4. Sharing an inquiry with a service provider

We will not share your identifying contact information with a specific independent contractor or service provider for the purpose of responding to your request unless you have first been clearly informed of the proposed sharing and have provided the required authorization. Any authorization process will identify the provider or otherwise explain the recipient and the information to be shared before the information is disclosed.

Service providers who receive an authorized inquiry may contact you about the service you requested. Their handling of information after they receive it is subject to their own privacy practices and applicable law.

5. Service providers and third parties

We may use technology providers to host the website, process inquiries, maintain records, or support communications. Those providers may process information on our behalf to provide their services. We do not sell personal information as a standalone data product through this website. This statement does not replace any specific disclosures or choices that may be required by applicable law.

6. Communications

If you provide contact information and submit a request, we may contact you about that request using the contact details you supplied. Any consent for calls or text messages, including automated communications where applicable, will be presented separately and clearly when required. Submitting a general inquiry should not be treated as blanket consent to unrelated marketing messages.

7. Data security and retention

We intend to use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information handled through the service. No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, operational recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

8. Your choices and requests

You may ask us to correct or delete information you have submitted, or ask questions about how it has been handled, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. A dedicated privacy contact method will be added before launch.

9. Website terms

Website content is provided for general information. Submitting an estimate request is not an offer, service contract, booking confirmation, or guarantee that a provider is available. Any scope, price, schedule, cleaning method, and service terms must be confirmed directly before work begins.

Descriptions of services are illustrative. Actual suitability depends on the property, surface material, condition, access, and other project-specific factors. Nothing on this website should be read as a promise of a particular result.

10. External websites

This website may eventually link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of websites we do not operate. Review their terms and privacy notices before providing information.

11. Changes to these policies

We may revise these policies as the service and its operating arrangements develop. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Material changes will be presented as required by applicable law.

12. Contact

Before launch, this section will be updated with the business mailing address and a dedicated email or other reliable method for privacy and policy inquiries.
